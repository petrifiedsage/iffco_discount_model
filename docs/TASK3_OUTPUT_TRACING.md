### Why Model Outputs Aren't Generated:

The ML model training (lines 700-726) might fail silently if:
1. Not enough training data (y.nunique() < 2)
2. Not enough positive/negative samples (label_counts.min() < 2)
3. Other training errors

**Evidence:**
